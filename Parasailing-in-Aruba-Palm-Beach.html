Parasailing Experience Highlights:

  • Standard Parasailing Experience: $70 per person
  • Parasailing with Photographer: $20 upgrade read more
  • Participants must be at least five years old
  • Each flight lasts around 10 min (total 45min - 1hr trip)
  • Weight Constraints: Parasailing requires a minimum combined weight of 180 lbs and a maximum capacity of 400 lbs.
  • Parasailing flights can reach up to 400ft high
  • Up to 3-person parasailing flights are possible.
    • Add as Preferred Source

Choose Your Parasailing Adventure:

  • Single Parasail Flight: In our Single flight, you go up in the parachute on your own. Just sit back relax and enjoy the amazing scenery!
  • Tandem Parasail Flight: Bring a friend and two of you sit next to each other in the parachute wearing your own separate harnesses. Going Tandem has never been more fun!
  • Triple Parasail Flight: Three-person parasailing adventure! Perfect for large groups, friends, and family. This is a experience not to be missed!

ARRIVING FOR YOUR TOUR:

  • Arrive 10 minutes early.
  • Check in at the Skyview beach desk.
  • Show your booking confirmation.
  • Staff confirms your activity.
  • Board / launch.
  • Safety briefing.

Additional Information and FAQ

Due to the popularity of parasailing, booking in advance is recommended; availability can fill quickly. Skyview ensures every flight is safe, fun, and unforgettable.

Cancellation & Refund Policy
