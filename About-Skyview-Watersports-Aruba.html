About Skyview Watersports

We are a small, family-owned business that was built from the ground up with a passion for creating unforgettable experiences on the beautiful waters of Aruba. From day one, our goal has been simple: provide every guest with an exciting adventure while maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and customer service.

Safety is at the heart of everything we do. Aruba enforces strict safety regulations for water sports, including parasailing. We obtain the required licenses and comply with government standards. These regulations cover equipment maintenance, operational procedures, and emergency preparedness to ensure the safety of both visitors and crew. This strong regulatory environment creates a culture of safety that benefits every guest who takes to the skies with us.

Our experienced captains and crew bring years of training and hands-on experience to every flight. Their knowledge, attention to detail, and commitment to professionalism ensure that every parasailing adventure is conducted with care, confidence, and an added focus on your comfort.

We also invest in modern boats, harnesses, parachutes, and safety equipment that meet international safety standards. Our equipment is routinely inspected, carefully maintained, and replaced whenever necessary to ensure that wear and tear never compromises your safety.

Whether it's your first time parasailing or you're returning for another adventure, we're honored to be part of your Aruba vacation. Thank you for trusting our family to help create memories that will last a lifetime.
