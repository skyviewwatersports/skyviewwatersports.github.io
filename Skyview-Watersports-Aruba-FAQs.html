Booking, Prices, Rules and Additional Information

Frequently Asked Questions (“FAQ”) and other important things to know when you book your tour and activities with us.

How long will the parasailing take?

During the flight you will be taken up to about 400 to 600 feet. 

What is the age limit for parasailing?

Kids as young as 5 years old can go parasailing.

What is proper attire for parasailing?

Wear your beach outfit. Water shoes or flip-flops, which could be left on the boat when going up.

Do you take photos or videos during the tour?

Yes, upon booking a photographer will be arranged to be on board for you to take photos of you going up and up in the air. You will receive here from 15 - 20 good quality photos. These photos will be ready and sent to you by no later than 7:00PM on the day of your booking.


What should I bring with me?

For a comfortable and hassle-free experience, we recommend bringing sun protection, swimwear (if applicable), a towel, and personal items you may need during your watersports adventure.

What is the age limit for parasailing?

Kids as young as 5 years old can go parasailing.

Where are you located?

Here's how to find us !

1. We are next to the Moomba Beach Bar.
2. When on the beach at the Moomba walk to the Hadicurari Pier and with your back to the pier - walk to your left.
3. Walk passed the Yellow Building (Nos Clubhuis) to us at the red icon. By the first row of beige umbrellas.

Your safety is our number one priority

The island's leading parasailing tour provider Equipped with the most modern specialized parasailing boats on the island, the latest safety features and diligently adhering to Aruba's strict safety regulations for parasailing.

Fly SINGLE, DOUBLE or TRIPLE; the minimum weight limit per flight is 180 pounds and maximum weight limit per flight is 400 pounds:

- A SINGLE person can go alone if he/she weighs no less than 180 pounds and no more than 400
pounds.

- TWO people can go together if their combined weights is no less than 180 pounds and no more
than 400 pounds.

- THREE people can go together if their combined weights is no less than 180 pounds and no more
than 400 pounds.

The weight limit may vary slightly due to wind conditions.
Your safety is our number one priority, if the wind conditions or any other circumstance arises and the captain deems it unsafe for parasailing; Skyview will try to contact you to reschedule your booking.

Book online or (for last minute bookings) contact us through any other messaging source for a Scheduling Agent and we will try our best to accommodate you. We look forward to having you!
