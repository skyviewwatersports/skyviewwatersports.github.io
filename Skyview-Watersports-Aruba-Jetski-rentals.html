 Jet Ski Rental Information:

Our jetski rentals are perfect for couples, friends, birthdays, bachelor & bachelorette outings. If raw horsepower is what excites you, then be ready for a pure adrenaline rush.

Our experienced staff will show you how to operate the jetskis and will inform you for the necessary safety procedures. We look forward to having you!

Open daily from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM for daytime rentals.
**ONLINE BOOKINGS are done with a USD 15.00 down payment.
The rest of the amount (USD 70.00) is to be paid upon arrival.

JETSKI HALF HOUR RENTAL :

Enjoy 30 mins on a jet ski or upgrade your options for a better EXPERIENCE

1 person on the jet ski is USD 85.00, and 2 persons on the jet ski are still USD 85.00. Rental is per jet ski not per passenger. Weight limit is 280lbs (Combined weight of both passengers if riding double)

-Must be 16 years or older to ride a jet ski (authorized by an adult).
-Kids younger than 16 years old can ride as a passenger with an adult.

What’s Included With Your Rental

Every jet ski rental includes:

  • Coast Guard-approved life jackets
  • Professional safety and operating briefing
  • Fuel for hourly rental use
  • On-water storage for phones and belongings
  • Your jet ski will be water-ready at launch
